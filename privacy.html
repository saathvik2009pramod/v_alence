Privacy

This notice describes data handling by the current Valence research prototype.

Simulation inputs

The interactive models run in your browser. The application does not send slider values, compensation inputs, or simulation results to a Valence server. These inputs are held in page memory and reset when the page is reloaded. The company deal-analysis page can read a CSV selected from your device. Its records remain in page memory and are not uploaded to a server. Reloading the page or using Clear imported records clears them. Downloaded cohort reports are saved to your device. The site currently offers no account registration, payment, or contact form.

Cookies, analytics, and storage

The Valence application code does not set cookies, install analytics or advertising trackers, or store simulation inputs in browser storage. Fonts, scripts, images, and styles are served with the site rather than loaded from third-party asset services.

Hosting and access

Hosting infrastructure may process technical information needed to deliver and protect the site, including IP address, requested URLs, browser information, and access times. A private preview may require authentication through its hosting provider. That access layer is separate from the Valence simulation. Processing and retention by the hosting or authentication provider are governed by that provider’s practices.

For a Vercel-hosted copy, see Vercel’s privacy policy. For a ChatGPT-hosted private preview, see OpenAI’s privacy policy.

External links

Opening a research or other external link takes you to another service, which can receive technical information from your browser. Valence sets a no-referrer policy to limit information shared through the referrer header.

Changes and questions

If features such as uploads, accounts, saved scenarios, or analytics are introduced, this notice should be updated to reflect the actual processing before those features are enabled. Contact the creator through the channel through which the site was shared for questions about this prototype’s data handling.
