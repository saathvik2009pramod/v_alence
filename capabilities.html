Calculation trace

Inspect the path
from rule to result.

Every result follows a defined sequence. Deal revenue and fixed cost determine gross contribution. A 3% sales commission and the incremental bonus determine rep pay. The model weights the early-close and fallback outcomes by their probabilities.

Company contribution deducts both compensation components. This makes it possible to distinguish a larger payout from a larger economic benefit.

What the laboratory reports

Expected rep pay change, expected company contribution change, early-close chance, quota attainment on a successful early close, and the preferred actions found in the bounded search.

How the smooth schedule is defined

Between 90% and 110% attainment, normalized position t follows bonus = target × (3t² − 2t³). This is the cubic Bézier with control points (0,0), (⅓,0), (⅔,1), (1,1). Below that range the bonus is zero; above it the bonus is capped.

How to reproduce a result

Set the six inputs in the interactive laboratory at the end of this page, select a payout rule, and run the decision search. The source model is included in the downloadable website files and uses deterministic expected-value calculations.

Available in this prototype

  • Six configurable deal and compensation inputs.
  • Three bonus structures and comparative payout curves.
  • Two-quarter expected-value accounting.
  • Animated search over a defined action set.
  • Separate employee and company objectives.

Product development direction

The proposed broader product would ingest real compensation plans, represent company-specific actions, produce reproducible conflict reports, and test constrained rule changes. Those workflows require further implementation and validation.

The current prototype evaluates a single deal with fixed costs, no time discounting, and an assumed response to discounts. It does not estimate employee behaviour, parse uploaded contracts, or automatically repair a compensation plan.
