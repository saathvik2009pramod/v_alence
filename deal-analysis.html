CSV columns and observation rules

Required columns are deal_id, decision_date, outcome, close_date, list_price, discount_pct, stage. Optional columns are quota_attainment_pct, deal_cost, rep_commission, rep_bonus, actual_sale_price.

  • Dates use YYYY-MM-DD. Outcomes are won, lost or open. Won and lost records need a close_date on or after decision_date.
  • Use numeric prices and percentages without currency symbols or separators. Optional financial fields are nonnegative amounts.
  • decision_date is the historical point where the quoted discount and stage were known. Use one row per deal.
  • We measure closure by the end of the decision's calendar quarter and by the end of the following calendar quarter. Fiscal calendars and later snapshot updates are outside this version.
  • Rates use cohorts whose full observation window has elapsed by the selected completeness date. Financial totals use actual won records closed by that date.
  • actual_sale_price is the recorded selling price for a won deal. Keep discount_pct as the quote at the decision snapshot, even if the final price changed.

Actual closed-deal economics

Recorded rep pay = commission + bonus. Recorded deal contribution = actual sale value − recorded deal cost − recorded rep pay. Only won deals with all four optional financial fields enter this calculation. Confirm those fields contain the compensation and costs attributable to that deal.

What this establishes

This workspace calculates historical frequencies and recorded deal economics. A recommendation about changing a discount or compensation plan still requires a defensible estimate of how that change would affect closing behaviour. Differences between historical groups can reflect customer difficulty, deal stage or other factors.

The illustrative decision laboratory remains available for inspecting compensation rules under explicitly entered assumptions.
